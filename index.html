What I want you to know

Vanshika,

I don't want to make this about how many years have passed, or how long I've carried these feelings.

I just want you to know that the little boy who once stayed quiet because he didn't know how to say what was in his heart has grown up.

And today, he doesn't want to hide behind silence anymore.

I'm not asking you to promise me a lifetime. I'm not asking you to feel something just because I felt it first, or because I wrote a poem years ago.

I simply want the chance to know you more deeply, to make you smile, to be there for you, to share ordinary little moments, and to discover what we could become together.

I don't know whether our story was always meant to return to this page. But now that we're here, I would really love to see where the next chapter could take us.

And if you feel even a little of what I feel, I would love to take that step with you.

With all the words I couldn't say back then,
